# Electrical Engineering

Calculators and utilities for electrical-design checks, schedules, loads, cable and breaker comparisons, voltage drop, generators, UPS systems, and related engineering tasks.

Tools here support engineering judgment; they do not replace applicable standards, manufacturer data, or professional review.
